Who I Am

I'm Najmus Sakib Sizan, a Senior Software Support Engineer at BJIT Limited with a deep passion for research in Machine Learning, Blockchain, Post-Quantum Cryptography, and IoT technologies.

I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from Green University of Bangladesh with a CGPA of 3.73/4.00, followed by an M.Sc. from Jagannath University (CGPA 3.81/4.00). I am currently pursuing my MPhil in Computer Science at Jagannath University. My work has earned 120+ citations across 9 publications with an h-index of 5.

My research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence and real-world applications — from smart agriculture and IoT-driven systems to blockchain security and decentralized frameworks.

"I believe in building technology that bridges the gap between academic research and practical engineering — creating solutions that truly make an impact."
